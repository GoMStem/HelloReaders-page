# Recommended Reading Books and Strategies for Improving Middle School Students' English Skills

In today’s world, being proficient in English is a necessity rather than an option. One of the most effective ways for middle school students to enhance their English skills is through consistent reading. This article will provide a list of recommended books for middle schoolers along with effective ways to utilize them.

## Recommended Book List

### 1. Fantasy
**Harry Potter Series**: The Harry Potter series by J.K. Rowling offers an engaging storyline that makes learning English enjoyable. Each book is filled with diverse vocabulary and expressions, making it perfect for building one's lexicon.

### 2. Classic Literature
**The Adventures of Tom Sawyer**: Mark Twain's 'The Adventures of Tom Sawyer' is an excellent classic for middle school students. Not only is the plot captivating, but it also provides insights into the culture and society of the time.

### 3. Science/Non-Fiction
**I Am Malala**: The story of Malala Yousafzai highlights the importance of peace and the power of education. Through this inspiring narrative, students can enhance their English skills while gaining awareness of critical social issues.

### 4. Young Adult Fiction
**Wonder**: R.J. Palacio's 'Wonder' revolves around themes of acceptance and friendship, making it relatable for middle school readers. Encouraging students to write reflections after reading can also enhance their writing skills in English.

## Strategies for Utilizing Reading

### 1. Keep a Reading Journal
After finishing a book, ask your child to jot down thoughts or questions in a reading journal. This practice helps in retaining what they read while also improving their writing skills in English.

### 2. Join a Book Club
Creating a book club with friends can be a wonderful way to discuss books regularly. Sharing different perspectives fosters critical thinking and enhances English comprehension.

### 3. Engage with Programs like Hello Readers
Encourage participation in reading programs like 'Hello Readers'. These programs offer a variety of books and resources, allowing children to enjoy English learning in an engaging way.

Balancing school life with personal leisure time while increasing English reading is an excellent way to enhance your child’s English proficiency.
