# Recommended Reading Lists by Genre to Enhance Middle School English Reading Skills

Developing English reading skills is crucial for students, especially in our rapidly changing global society where English has become an essential communication tool. As parents of middle school children, you understand the importance of nurturing your child's reading abilities in English. In this article, we will introduce genre-based recommended books to help enhance middle school students' English reading skills.

## 1. Classic Literature
Classic literature is a must-explore genre for students learning English. For example, 'The Adventures of Tom Sawyer' and 'Pride and Prejudice' provide depth in terms of sentence structure and vocabulary. These works not only hold literary value but also greatly assist in building historical background knowledge.

## 2. Fantasy
The fantasy genre is often a favorite among students, making it a suitable choice for encouraging reading. The 'Harry Potter' series, for instance, stimulates incredible imagination and makes learning English enjoyable. Through various characters and settings, students can naturally acquire vocabulary and grammar.

## 3. Contemporary Fiction
Contemporary fiction reflects current society and can enhance students' empathy. Books like 'The Fault in Our Stars' convey the myriad feelings adolescents experience, adding enjoyment to the reading. Reading such novels allows students to learn English while contemplating significant life themes.

## 4. Biography and Autobiography
Biographies and autobiographies, which deal with the lives of real people, can capture students' interest. Books like 'I Am Malala' not only improve English reading skills but also awaken a sense of global citizenship. These texts help strengthen both background knowledge and English proficiency.

## 5. Business and Self-help Books
It's beneficial for even middle school students to read business and self-help books. 'The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People' is an excellent choice that both parents and children can read together. Such books greatly contribute to developing problem-solving abilities and independent thinking.

## 6. Graphic Novels
Graphic novels combine visual elements, making them accessible to students who are new to English. Works like 'Maus' and 'Persepolis' tackle complex themes while being structured in an easy-to-understand manner, helping students engage with reading in English.

## Building Reading Habits
To initiate English reading through recommended books, it’s vital to establish consistent reading habits. Encourage students to keep a reading journal or write book reviews. These strategies can significantly enhance your child's English reading skills.

After reading, make it a point to discuss the book together. Encourage them to share their thoughts and ask various questions to expand their thinking. This is a great opportunity to cultivate critical thinking skills through conversation.

To enhance reading skills, allow them to choose books that genuinely interest them. When children select books in genres they enjoy, they naturally become engrossed in reading and improve their English proficiency.
