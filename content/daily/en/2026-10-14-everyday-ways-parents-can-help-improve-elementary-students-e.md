# Everyday Ways Parents Can Help Improve Elementary Students' English Reading Skills

As global communication becomes more essential, English is not merely a foreign language but a necessary tool for effective communication. For elementary school students, developing English reading skills early on is vital. Here, we explore practical methods parents can adopt to expose their children to English in their daily lives.

## 1. Reading English Storybooks

One of the most excellent methods to enhance your child's reading skills is to read English storybooks together. English storybooks often contain simple and repetitive phrases that facilitate language learning. Choose engaging stories that match your child's age and interests. Start by reading together, and gradually encourage your child to read independently.

## 2. Conversing in English

Having conversations in English at home can be immensely beneficial. Start with light, everyday interactions. For instance, during breakfast, you might ask, 'What do you want to eat today?' Practicing commonly used phrases in English fosters natural exposure to the language. Begin with short sentences and gradually increase their complexity as your child becomes more comfortable.

## 3. Watching English Cartoons and Movies

Allowing your child to watch their favorite cartoons or films in English is another effective method. Switch the language on shows they already enjoy to English. Watching with subtitles can help them understand better. This experience aids in learning the language's intonation and pronunciation naturally. Additionally, you can ask simple questions about the movie to prompt discussion.

## 4. Utilizing English Songs and Chants

Children are particularly responsive to music, making English songs and chants a fun way to learn. Listening to and singing along with catchy English songs can effectively enhance reading skills. Discussing the meaning of song lyrics can also provide valuable insights. A memorable song can leave a lasting positive impression on their language experience.

## 5. Encountering Everyday English Vocabulary

Another method for integrating English into your home is by utilizing everyday vocabulary. For example, when cooking together, refer to English recipes. You could say, 'Let’s make a pizza together!' as you follow the recipe step-by-step, teaching the necessary English words for each stage. This approach helps build vocabulary through real-life experiences.

## 6. Leveraging English Learning Apps

In today’s digital age, utilizing English learning apps is a fitting approach. Gamified apps that your child enjoys can make language learning fun and engaging. With various topics available, parents can participate and bond with their children during the learning process. Encourage your child to develop a daily habit of learning a little bit through these apps.

In summary, there are numerous ways that parents can enhance their children's English reading skills in everyday life. Supporting your child to shed any fear of English and embrace the language naturally is essential.
