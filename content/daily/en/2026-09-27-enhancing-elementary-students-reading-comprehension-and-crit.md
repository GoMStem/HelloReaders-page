# Enhancing Elementary Students' Reading Comprehension and Critical Thinking Skills Through English Reading Activities and Strategies

Reading in English plays a crucial role in developing language skills for elementary students. Beyond merely consuming information, reading helps nurture children's cognitive and comprehension abilities. Particularly, engaging with English texts offers opportunities to cultivate critical thinking skills in a natural way. In this article, we will explore various activities and strategies that can aid children in enhancing their reading comprehension and critical thinking through English reading.

## 1. Theme-Based Reading
Encourage children to engage with reading through theme or topic-based books that spark their interest. For example, selecting a theme such as 'Friendship' or 'Environmental Protection' and reading various related books can be beneficial. This approach helps students grasp multiple perspectives on a single subject, stimulating critical thinking. Additionally, discussions with peers about their thoughts and contrasting viewpoints provide excellent opportunities for deeper understanding.

## 2. Post-Reading Activities
After reading a book, it’s helpful to engage students in activities like summarizing the content or reflecting on key characters. For instance, posing questions like 'Why do you think this character made that decision?' encourages students to understand the core ideas and think logically. Reconstructing the story in their own words is also an effective strategy.

## 3. Keeping a Reading Journal
Encourage students to regularly maintain a reading journal. This journal can include the book's title, author, and notes on their thoughts and reactions to what they read. By organizing their thoughts in this manner, students can develop their ability to assess and think critically about the material. Maintaining a reading journal provides an excellent chance for reflection and retrospection.

## 4. Creating Questions
Have students formulate questions about the material they read. The process of generating their own questions allows them to engage with the content more deeply. This can significantly enhance their critical thinking skills. Questions such as, 'What lessons do you think can be learned from this story?' or 'What are your views on this ending?' work well for this purpose.

## 5. Group Reading and Discussion
Alternatively, set up group reading and discussion sessions with peers. Group discussions present an excellent opportunity for exchanging diverse opinions and understanding different perspectives. Exposure to a broader range of viewpoints will promote critical thinking and foster a sense of cooperation among students.

## Conclusion
Through English reading, elementary students can improve their reading comprehension while developing critical thinking skills. Parents play an essential role in facilitating an environment where children can engage in various reading activities and find joy in reading regularly.
