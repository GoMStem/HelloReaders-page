# Creating a Reading Plan to Dramatically Improve Elementary Students' English Reading Skills

Reading skills are vital foundations for a student's overall academic success. For parents of elementary school children, establishing an effective reading plan is crucial. In this article, we will explore the significance of creating a reading plan and practical strategies to enhance your child's English reading capabilities.

## The Importance of a Reading Plan
A robust reading plan goes beyond merely reading books; it captivates the child's interest and makes reading enjoyable. By having a reading plan, a student can set goals and begin developing reading habits right from the moment they select a book. This practice not only improves their reading abilities but also nurtures critical thinking and analytical skills.

## Setting Reading Goals
The first step is to set specific reading goals with your child. For instance, you could aim to read at least four books a month—setting realistic and achievable targets is essential. Goals help children feel a sense of accomplishment and motivate them to dive deeper into reading.

## Choosing Books
Selecting the right books is crucial for engaging your child. Consider their reading level and interests when choosing literature. For elementary students, look for stories that are both intriguing and easy to comprehend. You can search for age-appropriate leveled books or visit the library together to let them experience the joy of choosing books themselves.

## Post-Reading Activities
Enhance understanding by incorporating post-reading activities. Encourage your child to keep a reading journal or summarize the stories they have read. Additionally, having family discussions about the book’s content can stimulate their critical thinking and communication skills simultaneously.

## Regular Feedback
To maximize the effectiveness of a reading plan, regular feedback is vital. Discuss the books you’ve read together or evaluate the progress on your reading goals. This feedback allows your child to recognize their advancements and helps direct their future reading journey.

## Cultivating a Reading Habit
Finally, cultivating a reading habit requires consistency. Encourage your child to read at a designated time each day, and consider making trips to the library a weekend routine to integrate reading into their lifestyle. As a consistent pattern emerges, reading transforms from a chore to an enjoyable experience.

Thus, a structured reading plan is essential for enhancing English reading skills in elementary students. Assist your child in maintaining their interest in English by setting goals, selecting engaging books, incorporating post-reading activities, and providing regular feedback.
