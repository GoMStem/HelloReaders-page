# Reading Strategies to Improve Middle School English: Utilizing Different Genres

The middle school years are crucial for students to build a strong foundation in English skills. Reading not only enhances language comprehension but also improves expressive abilities. Therefore, it’s essential to explore various book genres to develop effective reading strategies. In this article, we will discuss diverse reading strategies that can help middle schoolers enhance their English skills and the types of books that best fit these strategies.

## The Importance of Diverse Genres
Books that students read should stimulate their interests and imagination. If students are only exposed to repetitive textbook content, it can quickly become dull. To keep reading engaging, it's essential to incorporate a variety of genres. Exposure to fiction, essays, poetry, and classic literature broadens the spectrum of their readings, which naturally leads to improvements in their English skills. Each genre presents unique language usage and styles, providing ample opportunities to learn new vocabulary and sentence structures.

## Recommended Genres and Books
1. **Fiction**: Engaging stories allow students to learn conversational English as they explore characters’ emotions and dialogues. For example, J.K. Rowling’s 'Harry Potter' series combines fantasy with themes of friendship and bravery, offering both inspiration and entertainment to students.
2. **Non-Fiction**: Books that delve into subjects like science, history, and social issues provide not only informative content but intriguing facts and data that broaden students’ background knowledge. Malcolm Gladwell’s 'Outliers' is a great example.
3. **Poetry and Literary Works**: Poetry serves as an excellent medium to encounter emotions and thoughts expressed in metaphorical language. Its concise format makes it less intimidating, allowing for creative interpretations that enhance critical thinking skills.
4. **Graphic Novels**: Combining visuals with text, graphic novels are a fantastic option for students who might struggle with language comprehension. Series like 'Percy Jackson' provide thrilling adventures and fantasy, which captivate readers.

## Reading Strategies
1. **Themed Reading**: Encourage students to choose a theme of interest and explore various genres related to that theme. This helps maintain engagement and motivates them to dive deeper into reading.
2. **Keeping a Reading Journal**: After finishing a book, students should jot down their reflections or insights gained. This practice boosts comprehension and aids in developing writing skills.
3. **Establishing a Reading Club**: Having students read books together and share their opinions provides an opportunity for open discussion. It fosters teamwork and empathy while allowing for diverse perspectives.

## Conclusion
Reading is an essential component of developing English skills during middle school years. It’s crucial for students to accumulate rich language experiences through a variety of genres and explore ways to enhance their skills without losing interest.
