# Effective Mixed Genre Reading Strategies to Improve Middle Schoolers' English Skills

For middle school students, improving their English reading skills is essential for academic success. One effective method is the mixed genre reading approach, which not only increases motivation but also enhances reading comprehension. Below, we will explore the components and benefits of this mixed genre reading strategy.

## Exploring Various Genres

Many parents often limit their children to novels or textbooks, which can narrow their reading experiences. However, exposing students to a variety of genres—such as fiction, non-fiction, poetry, graphic novels, and essays—can greatly enrich their vocabulary and comprehension skills. For instance, reading science fiction can help grasp scientific concepts, while historical fiction can make historical events more relatable.

## Advantages of Mixed Genre Reading

1. **Sparking Interest**: Diverse genres keep children engaged and prevent boredom. If a novel feels tedious, a graphic novel can provide an enjoyable narrative experience that is easier to follow.
   
2. **Expanding Vocabulary**: Each genre employs different vocabulary and sentence structures, thus broadening students' lexicons. Reading a newspaper article, for example, introduces practical vocabulary that students can use in their daily lives.
   
3. **Cultivating Critical Thinking**: Engaging with various genres encourages students to form their viewpoints and develop critical thinking skills. Holding discussions based on an essay they read can further reinforce these skills.

## Implementing Mixed Genre Reading

The mixed genre reading method includes a flexible and engaging set of activities. As parents, creating a reading list together and sharing reviews can enhance the experience. Consider conducting a summer reading project where you both read a range of genres. For example, after finishing a novel, you might watch a documentary film related to its themes—this can deepen understanding and enjoyment.

## Real-Life Example

Take the case of a middle school student, Student A, who committed to reading 2-3 books from various genres each week. This reading plan, which included novels, history books, and science articles, made reading enjoyable for him, and he saw improvements in his English test scores as a result. Inspired by the list suggested by his parents, Student A became proactive in his reading habits and maintained his enthusiasm.

## Concluding Thoughts

The mixed genre reading strategy offers students valuable new reading experiences while significantly improving their English language skills. By developing a habit of reading a little every day, middle schoolers can make monumental advancements. Moreover, taking time to discuss the books with your child can foster better communication skills as you exchange thoughts and insights.
