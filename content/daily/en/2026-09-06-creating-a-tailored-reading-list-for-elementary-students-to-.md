# Creating a Tailored Reading List for Elementary Students to Improve English Reading Skills

Learning English goes beyond just mastering vocabulary and grammar. Reading comprehension plays a vital role in understanding and effectively using the language. For elementary students, engaging with various reading materials is crucial to developing their English reading skills. In this article, we will introduce a tailored reading list designed to improve reading skills and effective ways to utilize these resources.

## Recommended Books by Theme

### 1. Fantasy
Fantasy books provide young readers with opportunities to unleash their imagination. Here are some recommended titles:
- 'Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone' by J.K. Rowling
- 'The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe' by C.S. Lewis

These stories not only captivate young hearts but also introduce a wider range of vocabulary in a fun context.

### 2. Friendship and Social Skills
Books that help children understand friendships and foster social skills:
- 'Charlotte's Web' by E.B. White
- 'Wonder' by R.J. Palacio

These titles contain valuable lessons about navigating social interactions and building friendships, providing insights that students can apply in real life.

### 3. Science Non-Fiction
Books that can pique students' curiosity about science:
- 'The Magic School Bus: Inside Ralphie' by Joanna Cole
- 'National Geographic Kids: The Most Fun Quote Book Ever'

Such books relay fascinating scientific facts, and discussing these topics afterward can greatly enhance their comprehension.

### 4. History and Culture
Learning history through engaging books is equally important:
- 'Who Was Anne Frank?' by Isabel Sanchez Vegara
- 'The Story of America' by K. Class

Students can learn about historical figures and events through these titles, encouraging deeper thinking and understanding of the world.

## How to Engage with Reading

Engaging with reading can go beyond merely finishing a book. Here are some strategies to implement:
- **Discuss the Book:** After reading, ask your child questions about the story. Questions like 'Why did this character act that way?' can stimulate critical thinking.
- **Post-Reading Activities:** Encourage your child to draw illustrations or summarize the story. This reinforce recall and comprehension of what they've read.
- **Reading Journals:** Have your child keep a log of the books they read along with their thoughts. This will not only improve their English writing skills but also promote reflective thinking.

In conclusion, we've explored various thematic books and ways to engage with reading to enhance English reading skills. Reading together with your child can be a wonderful bonding experience and a great way to motivate them in their learning journey.
