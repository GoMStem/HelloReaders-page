# 7 Creative Games and Activities to Motivate Elementary Students in English Reading

Reading in English provides students with essential foundational skills. For elementary students, learning English in a fun and engaging manner is crucial. Here are various games and activities to help children feel motivated to read in English.

## 1. Storytelling Card Game
This game utilizes a set of cards depicting various characters and places. Children draw cards and create stories based on them. This activity encourages creativity and helps them practice constructing English words and sentences. Adding discussions where children share their stories and exchange ideas can enhance engagement.

## 2. Reading Treasure Hunt
Prepare a list of specific words related to certain books or themes. Have the children find these words within the texts and mark the pages where the words appear. This approach keeps them focused on reading while developing their ability to actively search for information.

## 3. Translation Game
Divide into teams with friends and play a game where you translate sentences from Korean to English or vice versa. The team that accurately translates the most sentences within a set time wins. This activity boosts vocabulary and grammar knowledge while also being competitive and fun.

## 4. Create a Book
Work with children to create stories and turn them into a book. Choose a simple theme, and let each child write a section of the narrative that connects to the other parts. Afterwards, add illustrations and compile it into a book. The resulting creation will be something the child can proudly show off at home.

## 5. Reading Journal
Encourage students to maintain a reading journal where they write short reviews or reflections on the books they've read. This helps them organize their thoughts and improve writing skills. Regularly sharing these journals allows them to exchange feedback and appreciate each other’s perspectives.

## 6. Role Play
Select an engaging story and divide the roles among the children to enact it like a play. This activity promotes expressive skills and empathy while helping them naturally pick up pronunciation and intonation in English.

## 7. Visit an English Library
Take a trip to a nearby English library or bookstore and encourage them to find books they enjoy. Additionally, talking to library staff provides opportunities to learn new words and spark greater interest in reading.

These games and activities make English reading more enjoyable and approachable for children. If parents participate or encourage their children, the results will be even more effective.
