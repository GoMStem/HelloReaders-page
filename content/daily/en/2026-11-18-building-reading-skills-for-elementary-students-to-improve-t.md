# Building Reading Skills for Elementary Students to Improve Their English Proficiency

In English education, reading skills are crucial. The elementary school years are especially important for laying the foundation of language. Many parents may ponder how they can utilize reading activities to enhance their children's English skills. In this article, we will explore new types of reading and strategies for developing your child’s reading abilities.

## 1. Introducing Various Types of Reading

Recent studies suggest that trying out various types of reading can significantly enhance a child’s English reading skills. Here are a few beneficial reading types that you might consider:

### 1-1. Listening and Reading Aloud

Listening and reading involves not just reading text but also hearing it read aloud. Parents can read to their children, helping them grasp the story's flow easier and learn word pronunciation naturally. This method creates an engaging reading experience while simultaneously developing comprehension skills.

### 1-2. Using Picture Books

Picture books are an excellent way to help children understand stories visually before encountering complex sentences and vocabulary. Through illustrations, children can imagine the story and develop curiosity about the characters and events. Allowing children to choose their own picture books can foster their interest and love for reading.

### 1-3. Interactive Reading

Interactive reading is not merely about reading aloud but involves expressing emotions and differentiating characters' voices. This approach teaches rhythm and emotion in language while making reading a fun family activity.

## 2. Establishing Reading Strategies

In addition to experimenting with different reading types, establishing effective reading strategies is vital.

### 2-1. Setting Reading Goals

Collaborating with your child to set reading goals can be a rewarding process. For example, you might agree on reading two books per week. Providing small rewards as milestones are achieved can motivate them. This sense of achievement and joy in reading is essential for their development.

### 2-2. Keeping a Reading Journal

Writing a reading journal with your child can be instrumental in analyzing and managing their reading habits. Ask them to note the titles of the books they read, briefly summarize the plots, and express their thoughts or feelings about the stories. This practice nurtures their critical thinking and communication skills.

### 2-3. Discussion Time

After reading, take some time to discuss the content together. Encourage your child to articulate their thoughts and share your perspectives as well. This exchange is valuable for developing critical thinking and social interaction skills.

## 3. The Role of Parents

Parents play a vital role in their child’s English education. Observe your child's reading habits and attitudes, provide positive feedback, and show genuine interest in their activities. Sometimes children may lose interest, so it’s beneficial to offer diverse new materials and spend reading time together.

## Conclusion

Improving reading skills in English makes a significant contribution to your child’s overall proficiency. By leveraging different reading types and strategies, you can ignite their interest and enhance their reading capabilities through continuous guidance. Establishing solid reading habits serves as a fundamental basis for lifelong English proficiency.
