# Fun Strategies to Boost Your Child's English Reading Skills with Reading Programs

In today's globalized world, having strong English reading skills is crucial for students. Being able to communicate fluently in English goes beyond just academics; it's often tied to future career opportunities. Therefore, enhancing your child's reading ability is vital. This article outlines various engaging reading programs and practical methods for parents to utilize.

## Why is Reading in English Important?

Reading in English allows children to immerse themselves in the language more naturally. Instead of memorizing grammar rules or vocabulary, they learn to understand language patterns used in real-life contexts, gaining the ability to infer meanings from context. Additionally, reading a variety of topics helps develop critical thinking skills and enhances cognitive abilities.

## Recommended Reading Programs
1. **RAZ-Kids**  
   RAZ-Kids is an online reading program designed for elementary school students. It offers a range of books across different levels. Children can select books that match their reading proficiency and complete quizzes to test their understanding. Additionally, the program features audio reading, allowing them to hear native pronunciation as they learn.

2. **Epic!**  
   Epic! is an extensive platform that boasts a vast library of children's books, videos, and audiobooks. Your child can freely choose interesting topics, which increases their motivation to read. It's accessible on mobile devices, making reading convenient anytime, anywhere.

3. **Scholastic Reading Club**  
   The Scholastic Reading Club provides a recommended reading list featuring the latest must-read books for kids. It's easy for parents to purchase books at home, exposing children to different genres and enriching their reading experiences.

## Activities to Enhance Reading Together
- **Discuss the Books Read**  
   After your child reads a book, encourage discussions about the storyline or their favorite parts. This enhances comprehension and fosters critical thinking skills.
- **Create a Reading Journal**  
   Prompt your child to summarize the books they’ve read and jot down their thoughts in a reading journal. This practice improves focus on reading and significantly boosts their reading skills.
- **Family Reading Time**  
   Set aside regular family time where everyone reads their chosen books together. Sharing reading experiences fosters a greater enjoyment of reading for your child.

## Conclusion

Reading in English is an effective way to improve your child's reading skills. By utilizing various programs and actively participating as a parent, your child can engage with the language more enthusiastically.
