# Fun Reading Apps and Sites for Middle Schoolers

In today's world, English has established itself as the primary language of global communication. Helping your middle school child improve their English reading skills not only supports their academic success but also enriches their ability to connect with diverse cultures.  
In this article, we'll explore various apps and websites that allow middle school students to enjoy learning English through reading.

## 1. Epic!

Epic! is a digital library designed specifically for children and teens that makes reading in English enjoyable. With a vast array of books available at their fingertips, your child can explore various subjects and reading levels.  
They can choose captivating stories and read at their own pace. Notably, the app also offers an audiobook feature to enhance listening skills simultaneously.

## 2. Newsela

Newsela transforms current news articles into accessible reading for students. With a plethora of articles on different subjects being updated daily, your child can choose content suited to their reading level.  
Reading the news helps foster an interest in current events, and follow-up activities or quizzes are provided to assess their comprehension after reading.

## 3. Vocabulary.com

Vocabulary.com is an excellent app for enhancing vocabulary through engaging games. Your child can expand their vocabulary naturally via various quizzes and games.  
With the ability to select words based on their preferred topics or difficulty levels, learning becomes more personalized for them.

## 4. ReadTheory

ReadTheory provides personalized reading practice online. Your child receives tailored materials based on their reading level and can gradually improve their skills through practice questions designed to enhance reading comprehension, grammar, and summarization abilities.  
This tool is beneficial for developing a well-rounded reading skill set.

## 5. Storybird

Storybird is a creative platform where your child can craft and share their own stories. By allowing them to create in English, they can enhance both their writing and reading skills.  
Additionally, they can use various artwork to make their stories more appealing, sparking their creative thinking.

## Conclusion

These apps and websites serve as valuable tools for fostering your middle school child’s English reading abilities in an enjoyable way. It's essential to encourage a genuine interest in foreign languages, allowing children to engage with English naturally.
