# Utilizing Tailored Reading Supplement Materials for Elementary Students to Improve English Skills

In today's globalized world, English is an essential language that can greatly impact your child's future. The elementary years are critical for building a strong foundation in English. In this article, we will explore how to effectively use tailored reading supplementary materials to enhance your child's English skills.

## 1. Selecting a Variety of Reading Materials

It's essential to choose a range of reading materials that align with your child's interests. For instance, if your child is fascinated by animals or space, consider selecting English storybooks or science books related to these themes. The engaging topics will naturally spark their interest and allow them to learn numerous words and expressions during the reading process.

## 2. Creating a Customized Reading Planner

To help your child’s learning process, creating a reading planner can be beneficial. Write down a list of books to read weekly or monthly and encourage your child to summarize the content or write a reflection after finishing a book. This activity helps them better understand and retain what they have read.

## 3. Linking Activities After Reading

Engaging in activities related to the book after reading can enhance your child's comprehension. For example, drawing illustrations from the book, role-playing scenes, or creating stories can provide different perspectives on the material they have read. These interactive activities can further enrich their learning experience.

## 4. Utilizing Easy English Readers

Easy readers with adjustable difficulty levels are also excellent choices. 'Children's English readers' are often composed of short and concise sentences, making them accessible for elementary students. Along with regular reading, discussing the content or creating visual aids based on the stories will contribute to improving their English skills.

## 5. Establishing Regular Reading Time

Encouraging a daily reading routine is crucial. Designate at least 20-30 minutes a day for reading and consider spending this time with family. Discussing the books you've read and sharing thoughts can enhance not only your child's English skills but also strengthen family bonds.
